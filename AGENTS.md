## What Nivelato Labs is

Nivelato Labs is an AI-driven software lab based in San Juan, Puerto Rico, founded by Benj Lebron. Its slogan is "Software that measures up." The lab designs, builds, and ships production software using multi-agent development workflows: coordinated AI agents implement, test, and document in parallel, and a human directs every release.

## Products

- Nivelato: field measurement and quoting software for glass shops. Measure on site, sync to the shop, quote on the spot. Android and web. Product details and plans: https://nivelatolabs.com/products/ and https://nivelatolabs.com/pricing/
- PhoneBench: the first benchmark for fully autonomous skilled work. A model operates a robotic arm on a bench of powered tools and repairs a phone, scored against three quality tiers (Normal, High, Max), with full transcripts published for every run. Details, scenarios, and results: https://nivelatolabs.com/phonebench/ and the open source repository at https://github.com/legobele/phonebench
