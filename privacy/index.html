Nivelato Privacy Policy

What data the app collects, why, and how it is handled.

← Help Center

Nivelato is a mobile measurement tool. This document describes what data the app collects, why, and how it is handled.

1. Data collected

  • Measurements (jobs): yes, main function: saving field measurements. Stored in Firebase, linked to your account.
  • Email: yes (when creating an account), account identity, device linking.
  • Device identifier (derived UUID): yes, subscription restoration, device limits. Stored as a hash.
  • Approximate region: yes (IP geolocation, time zone, SIM country), plan availability.
  • Device posture (VPN state, integrity check, anti-emulator score): yes, periodic license integrity verification.
  • App version, device model, OS: yes, diagnostics.
  • Usage statistics (save count): yes, quota control by tier.

2. How the data is used

  • Main service: measurements are saved on your device plus a Firebase backup. You can delete any measurement or request deletion of your whole account from the app (see §3).
  • Periodic integrity verification: the app runs a check every 5 minutes while it is in use ("Backup and security verification" service). It sends the device UUID and the device posture (VPN state, integrity check, emulator detection). Purposes: (a) back up your measurements and (b) verify license integrity.

Data is transmitted encrypted (TLS). It is not sold.

3. Third parties data is shared with

  • ipapi.co: the app sends your IP address to ipapi.co to detect the country and show you the correct initial screen (routing only). Your name, email, and measurements are not sent.
  • ipinfo.io: our servers (the flowGate function) send your connection IP to ipinfo.io to detect VPN, proxy, or relay as part of license integrity verification. Your identity is not sent.

Outside these two IP geolocation providers, data is not shared with third parties.

4. Data deletion

  • You can delete any individual measurement from the app.
  • To delete your complete account: go to Settings → My account → "Request account deletion". Your request stays pending until a workshop administrator approves it (Workshop tab → "Deletion requests"). When it is approved, our servers automatically delete:
    • your profile document,
    • all your measurements (jobs) and their annotated photos,
    • your authentication account (email and sign-in credentials).
    There is no instant deletion without administrator approval.
  • Data saved only on your device (local cache) is deleted by uninstalling the app.
  • For data or deletion requests you can also write to support@nivelatolabs.com.

5. Data retention

  • Measurements: they are kept in the cloud while your account is active; when you delete your account they are erased (see §4).
  • Web pricing model (outside Quality Glass): $29 per major version per workshop + $4/user/month, with a free trial of 30 days or 200 saves.
  • QGC: corporate access for Quality Glass employees in Puerto Rico; the company provides it, it is not sold separately.
  • External tiers (e.g. Ultra outside Puerto Rico): they follow the web pricing model.

6. Privacy of minors

Nivelato is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Contact

For data requests, deletion, or questions: support@nivelatolabs.com

Need help using the app? Visit the Nivelato Help Center.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated; the app "Settings" screen always links to the current version.
