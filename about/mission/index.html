Mission

Software that measures up, for the trades that get ignored.

Nivelato Labs exists to build the software the big platforms never get around to: precise, field-ready tools for small shops doing skilled, physical work. Nivelato turns field measurements into quotes without the paper chase. PhoneBench keeps the lab honest about what its own methods can and cannot do.

The method is part of the mission. The work is built by coordinated AI agents, directed by a human who uses the software and answers for it, and verified against real results before it ships. If a product does not earn its place in the field, it does not ship.
