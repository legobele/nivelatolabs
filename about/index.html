About

An AI-driven lab, on purpose.

Nivelato Labs is a software lab in Puerto Rico founded by Benj Lebron. It runs on a simple bet: a small lab that masters agentic development will out-ship a large team that does not.

The model

Agents build. A human directs.

Multi-agent development is the production line. Instead of one assistant answering one prompt at a time, the lab runs coordinated agents in parallel: one implements, one tests, one reviews, one documents. Work that used to queue up now compounds.

Direction stays human. Product calls, quality standards, and release decisions are made by a person who uses the software and answers for it. Agents propose and execute; the human disposes. Every release passes a human review, and every public claim traces back to a verifiable result.

Verification is a feature, not a chore. Agents check each other's work against source data before anything ships. Benchmarks like PhoneBench exist because the lab measures its own tools the same way it measures everyone else's: against a standard that does not bend.

The output is real. Nivelato runs in production at real glass shops. PhoneBench publishes full transcripts, including the failures. The lab's credibility is its distribution: do the work in public, show the receipts, and let the results rank.
