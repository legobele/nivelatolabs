The upside

Working with a team of one

A team of one is not a smaller version of a big team. It removes entire categories of failure, and what is left is one person who answers for everything.

01

You always talk to the founder

No account managers, no handoffs, no "let me check with the team." The person on the call is the person who decides.

02

Nothing gets lost between departments

There are no departments for things to get lost between. Everything the lab knows lives in one head, and it stays there.

03

The person who sells it ships it

Sales, engineering, and support are the same person, so a promise made on a call is a promise the roadmap already kept.
