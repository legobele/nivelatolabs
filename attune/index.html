Attune

An agent calibrated to one person. It does not just do the thing. It does the thing, and the thing behind the thing, in the way you would have done it on your best day.

This page shows a part of it. That is deliberate.

[ The surface ]
Nothing Everything

Attune Desktop is one surface with a dial. At one end: the chat, the model, a stopwatch, and you. At the other: every signal the model emits, down to a single token you can stop, replay, and steer. Where you live on that line is yours. The app learns it without asking.
