RESEARCH

PhoneBench

The first benchmark for fully autonomous skilled work. PhoneBench hands a model a robotic arm, a bench of powered tools, and a broken phone, then scores the repair against three quality tiers. Every scenario is a real repair, parameterized from published repair guides down to the last screw, and every run publishes its full transcript.
