Pay at
the machine.

There is no checkout on this page, and nothing here can charge you. Payment happens on the COIL machine itself: the screen shows a fresh code for your exact purchase, your phone pays, the snack drops. If you scanned your way here outside of a purchase, you are simply early.

  1. [ Step 01 ]Pick your snack on the machine screen.
  2. [ Step 02 ]The machine shows a pay code for that item and that total. It expires in 90 seconds; expired means no charge.
  3. [ Step 03 ]Scan it with your phone, pay, and collect below.
