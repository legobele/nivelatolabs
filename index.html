What it does

Everything the job needs, in your pocket.

Four capabilities in one app: field measurement, photo capture, project organization, and team management.

Field measurement

Digital capture for every opening, dimension, and note, room by room, right on your phone. No clipboards, no lost napkins, no retyping at the office.

  • Fast dimension entry built for gloves-on work
  • Notes and flags right on each measurement
  • Works offline, syncs when you are back on signal

Photo capture

Photos attach to each measurement automatically and stay with the job, so the shop sees exactly what you saw on site.

  • One-tap photo step inside the measurement flow
  • Photos stored with the job, not lost in a camera roll
  • Review shots later from the dashboard or the app

Project organization

Group measurements into projects. Track what is measured, what is quoted, and what is done, all in one place instead of scattered across chats and paper.

  • Projects with customers, users, and measurements
  • Full history per project, searchable any time
  • Dashboard view for the whole shop pipeline

Team management

Invite your crew and keep everyone on the same job list. Owners control who sees what, and permissions follow each person automatically.

  • Invite by code, approve from the dashboard
  • Role-based permissions: owners, supervisors, measurers
  • Shared projects with per-user access
How it works

Measure, sync, quote. Done.

A process, not a pile of tools: three steps in order, from the job site to the finished quote, in a single visit.

STEP 01

Measure on site

Open Nivelato, pick the project, and log dimensions as you walk the job. Photos optional, encouraged.

STEP 02

Sync to the shop

Measurements upload automatically. The office sees the job the moment you finish, no end-of-day data entry.

STEP 03

Quote and close

Turn the measurement set into a customer-ready quote before you leave the driveway.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Quick answers about the app, how it works, and the plans.

What is Nivelato?

Nivelato is a field measurement app for glass shops. You measure openings on site with your phone, the job syncs back to the shop, and you can quote on the spot instead of retyping notes at the office.

How does Nivelato work?

Three steps. Measure on site, let the measurements sync to the shop automatically, then turn the measurement set into a customer-ready quote before you leave.

Is Nivelato available now?

Nivelato is in closed testing on Google Play, and the full web app is available now. Sign in with your shop account or a personal account and start measuring.

Does Nivelato work without a signal?

Yes. Field measurement works offline, and everything syncs when you are back on signal.

Can my whole team use it?

Yes. Invite your crew by code, approve them from the dashboard, and use role-based permissions for owners, supervisors, and measurers. Projects are shared with per-user access.

How much does it cost?

There are Free, Pro, and Ultra plans. Plans are per shop and billed through Google Play. See the pricing page for what each tier includes.
