What we do

A lab, not a portfolio.

Everything here is a working product with real users, real constraints, and a public record. We build vertical software for trades that big platforms ignore, and we publish the research that comes out of building it.

01 · BUILD

Products that ship

Field-ready software for offset measurement, quoting, and operations, designed with the people who use it every day and priced for small shops.

02 · RESEARCH

Benchmarks that tell the truth

PhoneBench is a robotics AI benchmark: it measures what AI agents can actually do when the work is physical, sequential, and judged against an unforgiving standard.

Multi-agent workflows handle implementation, testing, and verification in parallel, while direction, taste, and final calls stay human.
