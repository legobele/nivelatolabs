Getting started

  1. Install Nivelato from the Google Play Store on your Android.
  2. Create your account, you can sign in with Google or with email and password.
  3. Start measuring. The free account includes 3 saves so you can try the full flow before paying.

Requirements

  • Android with Google Play Store and Google Play Billing.
  • A laser measure (recommended) or a tape measure to take the measurements.
  • Internet connection to sign in and sync; measuring works offline once you are in.

How to measure an opening

Every job follows the same 7 steps. Measurements are entered in inches with fractions (e.g. 36 ½").

  1. Customer / ProjectCustomer name, project, and location before you start.
  2. Opening measurementsWidth at the bottom and height on the left. The other two measurements are calculated automatically from the offsets.
  3. Left wallFrom the laser to the wall: point A (bottom) and point B (top).
  4. Right wallThe same on the other side: point A (bottom) and point B (top).
  5. TopOffset of the ceiling / top side.
  6. BottomOffset of the floor / bottom side.
  7. Photo with measurementsTake a photo of the opening with the measurements visible (requires a Pro plan or higher).
  8. SummaryReview everything, save the job, and share it if your plan allows it.

Tips

  • Always measure in the same order so you don't mix up points A and B.
  • If an offset reads zero at both points, the wall is plumb, perfect.
  • The step 7 photo saves you from arguments: always take it before leaving the site.

Subscriptions and cancellation

  • Subscriptions are bought with Google Play Billing and renew automatically.
  • To cancel or change your plan: open Play Store → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Nivelato → Cancel.
  • When you cancel, you keep access until the end of the paid period.
  • Pro Lifetime is a one-time payment; it does not renew.
  • Refunds are processed by Google Play according to its policy.

Pro or Ultra?

  • Pro is just for you: your measurements, your jobs, your phone.
  • Ultra is for teams: up to 30 users under a single subscription, with unique links to share jobs with the rest of the crew.

Account and data

  • Switching phones: sign in with the same account (Google or email) on the new device. Your Google Play subscription travels with you.
  • Deleting your account: inside the app, on the "Privacy and data" screen, you can delete your account, this erases your email, your team link, and your cloud data.
  • What we keep: your email (account identity), your device link, and your saved jobs. Full details in the privacy policy.
  • Forgot your password? Use "Forgot your password?" on the email login screen.

Common problems

I can't sign in with Google
Check that the phone has an internet connection and that date/time are set to automatic. If the error persists, clear the Google Play Services cache and try again.
I bought Pro but the app is still in free mode
Close the app completely and reopen it with internet, the app verifies your subscription at launch. If it still doesn't activate, confirm in Play Store → Subscriptions that the payment went through, and write to us with your account email.
My jobs don't appear / don't sync
Sync requires internet. Open the app while connected and wait a few seconds. If a job was saved offline, it appears when you reconnect.
The camera won't open at the photo step
Nivelato needs camera permission: go to Android Settings → Apps → Nivelato → Permissions → Camera → Allow.
The fractions don't add up for me
The app works in inch fractions (½, ¼, ⅛, 1/16). Enter the whole number and pick the fraction from the menu, don't type decimals into the fraction field.
The app closes when I open it
Make sure you have the latest version from the Play Store. If the problem continues, restart the phone. As a last resort, reinstall (your cloud jobs are not lost).
Does it work without internet?
Yes for measuring: once your session has started, you can complete jobs offline. You need internet to sign in, sync, and verify your subscription.

Versions and going back to an earlier version

Sometimes a company needs to stay on an earlier version or undo a version change. This is what can be done today:

  • Postponing an update: when a data update arrives, the company owner can postpone it from the app's updates page. The system applies it later, only when nobody in the company is connected, so data is never touched in the middle of a work shift.
  • Going back to an earlier interface label: every company runs with an interface version label. The owner can ask for their company to go back to an earlier label; the change moves the label, not the data.
  • No more channels: there is no beta channel and no stable channel anymore. Every company runs the current version of Nivelato and gets new features at the same time.
  • Seeing which version your company runs: at debug.nivelatolabs.com you can see your company's data version, its channel, and which version is being offered to it.

What doesn't exist (yet)

  • There is no automatic data rollback: company data migration is one-way by design. If your company needs its data returned to the previous version, it is a manual intervention by the Nivelato Labs team. It is requested with a support session: at remote.nivelatolabs.com, the owner enters the single-use code the support person gives them, and the session is recorded and time-limited.
  • Google Play doesn't downgrade: Play does not allow installing an older version of the app over a newer one on a phone. An older version can only be distributed through a test track.
